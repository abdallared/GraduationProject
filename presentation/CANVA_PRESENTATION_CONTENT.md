## 🎯 Slide 04: Introduction & The Hook (Story & Origin)
* **Slide Type:** Storytelling / Hook
* **Title:** **The Spark: True Independence for the Blind**
* **Subtitle:** Moving from passive guidance to active autonomous decision-making

### 📋 Slide Content Elements
* **The Inspiration Story:**
  - We asked: *Can a visually impaired person walk through an unknown environment without holding a white cane or waiting for someone else's commands?*
* **The Global Survey & Seventh Sense (7th Sense SuperBrain 1):**
  - We analyzed world solutions including the commercial 7th Sense system.
  - **The Revelation:** While 7th Sense proved tactile sensory feedback is clinically viable, it suffers from critical limitations: **exorbitant price tag ($thousands)**, bulky setup, and relies on passive operator instruction rather than empowering the user.
* **The Paradigm Shift — Autonomous Decision-Making:**
  - Blind individuals do *not* want a device that tells them "turn left, stop, walk right".
  - They want **spatial perception** — feeling where the objects are so **THEY take the decision themselves**.
* **Multimodal Synergy:**
  - Combining **Embedded Electronics + Edge AI + IoT Caregiver Telemetry** into an affordable, everyday wearable visor.

## 📚 Slide 06: Literature Review & The PRISMA Framework
* **Slide Type:** Scientific Rigor / Methodology
* **Title:** **Systematic Literature Review (SLR)**
* **Subtitle:** Rigorous evidence synthesis following PRISMA 2020 guidelines across 137 peer-reviewed studies

### 📋 Slide Content Elements
* **Scientific Foundations:**
  - **Tactile Sensory Substitution (TSS):** Encoding visual spatial pixels into mechanical pressure on sensitive skin.
  - **Neuroplasticity:** Brain rewires occipital visual cortex to decode tactile inputs into spatial cognitive maps (Paul Bach-y-Rita paradigm).
* **The PRISMA Pipeline Metrics:**
  - **Identification:** 3,104 initial records (1,759 Scopus + 1,345 Web of Science).
  - **Deduplication:** 874 cross-database duplicates removed → 2,230 unique records.
  - **Screening:** 2,230 screened by title & abstract → 2,080 excluded (no haptics / off-topic).
  - **Eligibility:** 150 full-text papers retrieved & critically reviewed → 13 excluded (duplicate files).
  - **Included Corpus:** **137 high-impact studies** analyzed and synthesized.
* **Corpus Clustering:**
  - Navigation & Mobility (17) | Tactile & Haptic Feedback (14) | Wearable Visor/Belt (10) | Depth Estimation (8) | Computer Vision (7) | Multimodal Fusion (6) | Neuroscience (4).

## ⚠️ Slide 07: Problem Statement
* **Slide Type:** Challenge Definition
* **Title:** **The Blind Spots of Existing Assistive Aids**
* **Subtitle:** Why traditional tools and current commercial gadgets fall short

### 📋 Slide Content Elements (3 Challenge Pillars)
1. **The White Cane Fallacy:**
   - Detects only ground-level obstacles within 1 meter.
   - Completely blind to **head-height hazards** (tree branches, hanging signs, open truck beds).
2. **Sensory Overload & Auditory Blocking:**
   - Existing smart glasses rely on audio buzzers or voice synthesizers.
   - **Critical Safety Risk:** Masks ambient traffic sounds, sirens, and human voices.
3. **The Commercial Barrier (Seventh Sense & Competitors):**
   - High cost ($3,000 – $10,000+), inaccessible in Egypt and developing nations.
   - Bulky backpacks, exposed tethered wiring, and lack of real-time caregiver emergency tracking.

### 🎙️ Speaker Notes
> *"The white cane is 100 years old. It cannot protect the user's upper body or head. Meanwhile, audio-based smart glasses block the ears — which is the blind person's most vital safety sense. Commercial alternatives are either unaffordable or mechanically impractical."*

## 🎯 Slide 09: Project Objectives
* **Slide Type:** Quantitative Targets
* **Title:** **Engineering Goals & Target Specifications**
* **Subtitle:** Concrete technical and clinical targets driving the Mechatronics design

### 📋 Slide Content Elements (4 Grid Cards)
* **Goal 1: Sub-50ms Real-Time Latency**
  - Complete loop: Camera capture → AI inference → Depth filtering → Pin matrix actuation in `<40ms` (30+ FPS) to match human tactile reflex speed.
* **Goal 2: 5×5 Spatial Resolution on Forehead**
  - 25 independent tactile pixels with variable stroke depth and frequency encoding obstacle proximity.
* **Goal 3: Custom PCB Miniaturization**
  - Designing a proprietary 2-layer PCB to house motor drivers, power regulation, and MCU into a lightweight visor `<250g`.
* **Goal 4: Connected Caregiver IoT Ecosystem**
  - BLE and Wi-Fi synchronization with smartphone app; instant fall detection and emergency SOS broadcast.

### 🎙️ Speaker Notes
> *"Our engineering specifications are defined by human biology: tactile reflexes require end-to-end latency under 50ms. We engineered every component — from camera frame rates to custom PCB driver cycles — to meet a 36ms total latency budget."*

## 🛠️ Slide 10: Proposed System Design (Hardware, AI, IoT)
* **Slide Type:** System Architecture
* **Title:** **Proposed Architecture: Multimodal Integration**
* **Subtitle:** Seamless synchronization of optical sensing, edge machine learning, and tactile actuators

### 📋 Slide Content Elements (3 Core Pillars)
1. **Perception & Hardware Layer:**
   - **Sensors Researched:** Stereo Vision (ZED 2i), Time-of-Flight ToF (OPT8241), and mmWave Radar (TI IWR6843 EVM).
   - **Tactile Actuators:** Miniature Voice Coil / Micro Linear Actuators (Thorlabs / PQ12-S) for instant response without mechanical lag.
   - **Custom PCB:** Designed via Flux.ai / KiCad to minimize wires and reduce weight.
2. **AI & Computer Vision Layer:**
   - **Model Backbone:** Lightweight YOLO / MobileNet fine-tuned on assistive navigation datasets.
   - **Transfer Learning:** Custom trained on navigation hazards (stairs, doors, curbs, obstacles).
   - **Tactile Encoding:** Disparity depth map quantized into 5×5 matrix with logarithmic distance mapping.
3. **IoT & Caregiver Mobile App:**
   - Real-time GPS location tracking for family/caregivers.
   - Fall detection telemetry and emergency automated alerts.

## 📊 Slide 11: Parametric Study & Results
* **Slide Type:** Empirical Evaluation
* **Title:** **Parametric Study & Benchmark Results**
* **Subtitle:** Validating detection accuracy and processing throughput against real-time benchmarks

### 📋 Slide Content Elements
* **Obstacle Detection Accuracy (mAP@0.5):**
  - **Pedestrians / Persons:** `91.2%`
  - **Vehicles / Bicycles:** `92.1%`
  - **Furniture & Indoors:** `89.3%`
  - **Stairs & Level Changes:** `88.5%`
  - **Curbs & Ground Holes:** `84.7%`
* **Real-Time Latency Breakdown (Target: <50ms):**
  - Preprocessing & Frame Grab: `8.2 ms`
  - Edge AI Inference: `18.5 ms`
  - Depth Filtering & Disparity: `4.3 ms`
  - Pin Matrix Driver Update: `5.1 ms`
  - **Total End-to-End Latency: `36.1 ms` (~28-32 FPS) — EXCEEDS SPECIFICATION!**

Lean Canvas: Business Architecture

Validated B2B2C economic engine and distribution model for visual-to-tactile assistive tech.

1. Problem

  • Severe spatial isolation (285M visually impaired).
  • Audio fatigue & ear blockage from talking screen readers (masks critical traffic sounds).
  • Clunky traditional canes fail on head-level hazards.
Existing Alternatives:

White cane ($30), Guide dog ($40k+), 7Sense SuperBrain ($12,000+).

2. Solution

  • Dual wide-angle stereo vision & depth sensors.
  • Real-time on-device edge AI (<100ms obstacle detection & depth mapping).
  • 5×5 abdominal vibrotactile haptic matrix conveying direction, proximity, and hazard level silently.

3. Value Proposition

"Spatial Sight Through Touch": Real-time 3D spatial awareness translated into silent tactile feedback.

High-level Concept:

"Autonomous Spatial Awareness for the Blind — Ear-Free, Hands-Free."

4. Unfair Advantage

  • Ear-Free safety patent-pending tactile mapping architecture.
  • 95%+ lower cost compared to European competitor 7Sense ($300 vs $12,000).
  • Proprietary low-latency edge tactile encoding algorithms.

5. Customer Segments

  • Primary: Blind & severely visually impaired individuals (40M worldwide).
  • Institutional Buyers: Rehabilitation NGOs, Special Education institutions, Ministry of Social Solidarity.
  • Early Adopters: Tech-savvy blind professionals and university students.

6. Key Metrics

  • Obstacle Avoidance Success Rate (>95%).
  • User Spatial Orientation Speed (+40% faster navigation).
  • System End-to-End Latency (<100ms benchmark).
  • Device Adoption & Monthly Active Usage rate.

7. Channels

  • B2B partnerships with blindness associations (e.g., Al Nour Wal Amal, Resala, Baseera).
  • Direct-to-Consumer e-commerce & assistive tech hubs.
  • Government disability subsidy & medical device inclusion programs.

8. Cost Structure

  • Bill of Materials (BOM): ERM/LRA vibration actuators, edge MCU/SoC, camera modules (~$85/unit at volume).
  • Manufacturing & Assembly (local fabrication & testing).
  • Edge AI inference optimization & R&D.

9. Revenue Streams

  • Hardware Sales: Core Haptic Belt + Vision Sensor kit ($249 - $349 target retail, subsidized via NGOs).
  • SaaS Navigation Upgrade: Cloud mapping, indoor spatial updates, custom AI hazard detection ($9.99/mo).
  • B2B Training & Maintenance support contracts.

## Slide 2: The Problem
* **Title:** **THE PROBLEM**
* **Subtitle:** Navigating the World with Visual Impairment
* **01 // White Canes Miss Overhead Hazards**  
  * Only scans ground level within 1 meter; misses branches, open windows, and signs.
* **02 // Audio Smart Glasses Block Hearing**  
  * Masks ambient street noise and approaching vehicles, creating critical safety hazards.
* **03 // High-Tech Western Solutions are Unaffordable**  
  * European devices like 7Sense and BrainPort cost $3,000–$5,000+, out of reach for emerging markets.

## Slide 3: The Solution
* **Title:** **THE SOLUTION**
* **Subtitle:** A Physical Sixth Sense for Navigational Independence
* **01 // Wearable 3D Depth Perception**  
  * RGB-D camera captures real-world 3D geometry in real time at 30 FPS.
* **02 // 5×5 Mechanical Pin Matrix**  
  * Translates distances into intuitive physical height profiles on the user's forehead.
* **03 // 100% Ear-Free & Hands-Free**  
  * Leaves ears open to ambient sound and hands free for normal daily life.

## Slide 4: How It Works (The 3 Pillars)
* **Title:** **HOW IT WORKS**
* **Subtitle:** Simple 3-Stage Pipeline from Light to Touch
* **Stage 1: The Eye (Input)**  
  * Wide-angle RGB-D depth camera capturing 3D spatial points.
* **Stage 2: The Brain (Edge AI)**  
  * YOLOv8 real-time obstacle detection + RANSAC 80 cm safe walking corridor.
* **Stage 3: The Skin (Output)**  
  * 25 micro-actuator pins modulating physical height levels (0 to 5) directly against the forehead.

## 🏷️ المحاور والتصنيفات المعتمدة للمشروع (Taxonomy Reference)

### 1. الـ Main Tags (المحاور الاستراتيجية الأربعة)
* 🎯 **`Tactile & Sensory Substitution Systems (TSSS)`**: أجهزة التعويض الحسي ومصفوفات النبضات/الدبابيس اللمسية.
* 🎯 **`Edge Computer Vision & 3D Spatial Perception (CV-3D)`**: كاميرات وحساسات العمق (RGB-D) ونماذج الذكاء الاصطناعي (YOLO).
* 🎯 **`Ground Segmentation & Safe Path Navigation (GS-PN)`**: تجزئة مستوى الأرضية وتفادي الحفر والسلالم والممرات الآمنة.
* 🎯 **`Cognitive Ergonomics & Blind Usability (CE-BU)`**: العوامل البشرية، الحمل الذهني، وتجربة المكفوفين بدون حجب السمع.

### 2. الـ Another Tags (الـ 6 توبيكس الأساسية المعتمدة)
* 🏷️ `Tactile-Actuation` | المشغلات الميكانيكية، المحركات اللمسية، ودقة المصفوفة.
* 🏷️ `Depth-Sensing` | كاميرات العمق، الستيريو، مستشعرات ToF، والـ LiDAR.
* 🏷️ `AI-Object-Detection` | نماذج YOLO وخوارزميات الرؤية الحاسوبية الخفيفة.
* 🏷️ `Corridor-Navigation` | المظروف الآمن (Safe Corridor) وتوجيه الحركة الداخلي.
* 🏷️ `Human-Factors-Usability` | الاختبارات السريرية والميدانية مع المكفوفين وسهولة التعلم.
* 🏷️ `Wearable-Embedded` | شكل الجهاز القابل للارتداء، المعالجات المدمجة (Raspberry Pi / ESP32 / Jetson)، استهلاك الطاقة وسهولة الحمل.

**المشكلة البحثية (Research Problem):**
> Visually impaired people face several difficulties in accessing information, navigation and travel, object recognition, and spatial perception. **الحل المقترح والمنهجية (Proposed Solution):**
> The paper argues that a single-sensory substitution approach is no longer sufficient to meet the diverse needs of visually impaired people across different environments. Each sense provides different and complementary information, so relying only on audio or only on tactile feedback may not be suitable for every situation. Therefore, the proposed solution is a multimodal interaction system based on sensory compensation theory, which combines multiple approaches, such as visual enhancement, auditory compensation, and tactile compensation, according to the user's needs and the interaction scenario.
The researchers designed a game-viewing system for visually impaired people, consisting of an application side and a device side. The system supports different functions such as tournament information, navigation, accessible viewing, device connection, object recognition, venue tours, and calling volunteers. They created the interactive interface prototype using Axure RP and tested it on smartphones.   
The system was tested with 6 visually impaired participants aged 20–40, including 2 blind participants and 4 visually impaired participants. The evaluation focused on user friendliness, effectiveness, fault tolerance, and satisfaction. The results were generally positive: effectiveness scored 4.6/5 and satisfaction scored 4.7/5. However, the researchers found that multiple steps in the interface could cause user errors, so they suggested improvements such as auditory guidance, confirmation pop-ups, and physical differentiation of interface areas. Overall, the proposed design largely met the researchers' expectations.

**المشكلة البحثية (Research Problem):**
> Traditional Tactile Vision Substitution Systems (TVSS) have limited tactile resolution compared with the large amount of information contained in visual images. Therefore, visual information cannot be directly transferred to the tactile device without processing and reducing its resolution. Traditional systems can also suffer from problems such as light reflection, noise, texture artifacts, and loss of object features. Moreover, because tactile resolution is limited, users need to actively move the camera to explore objects, making it important to understand their active sensing strategies

**الحل المقترح والمنهجية (Proposed Solution):**
> The researchers developed an improved Tactile Vision Substitution System (TVSS) that converts visual information captured by a camera into simplified tactile information that can be perceived through the fingers. The proposed system contains two main subsystems:
1. Image Processing Subsystem: reduces the resolution of camera images while preserving important object features and converts them into binary images suitable for the tactile stimulation device.
2. Trajectory Tracking Subsystem: tracks the movement of the active camera while the user explores objects, allowing researchers to analyze the user's active sensing strategy.
